The published NEO Maths site is where the canonical maths spine is actually taught: one interactive lesson page per lesson, each carrying the six Cornerstones, Reconnection Routes to earlier learning, a Practice Companion, a Learner Guide and Supporting Adult Guidance. Built and maintained by NEO's maths curriculum designer in [`nudgeeducation/neo-maths`](https://github.com/nudgeeducation/neo-maths); Git is canonical, the Google Classroom master courses are derived from it.

This page is the join between the vault and the site. The vault says *what* the learning is ([[../02-curriculum/ks3/maths/index|KS3 Maths]], [[../02-curriculum/ks4/maths/index|KS4 Maths]]) and *how it is assessed* ([[../03-overlays/edexcel-igcse/maths-a/index|Edexcel IGCSE 4MA1]]); the site holds the lessons.

## Pathways and rhythm (GreenPrint entry v2.2)

- **Foundations** (internal stage code Y9) — 2 × 45-minute lessons a week, 76 guided lesson opportunities a year; National Curriculum KS3 bridging to 4MA1 Foundation tier.
- **Intermediate** (internal stage code Y10) — 3 × 45-minute lessons a week, 114 opportunities; 4MA1 Higher tier, which assumes all Foundation content.

Stage codes are internal, not learner-facing year groups.
